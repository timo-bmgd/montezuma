Sampling is the exploration mechanism: even an action rated at 3% gets tried roughly every 33rd opportunity, so the agent keeps testing alternatives instead of locking onto its current favourite. An entropy bonus in the loss (Fig. 3) rewards keeping the distribution spread out. The two value heads take no part in choosing the action — their forecasts drive the learning update (Fig. 3).

2 · RND: curiosity as prediction error

3 · The learning update: every 128 steps

The agent learns while playing: after every 128 steps (per env ×32 envs = 4,096 samples) play pauses, the whole batch is turned into one update, play resumes.

GAE is a backward recursion (t = 127 → 0): Aₜ = δₜ + γλ·Aₜ₊₁, with δₜ = rₜ + γV(sₜ₊₁) − V(sₜ). A reward reaches back and raises the advantage of the actions that led to it — credit assignment over the whole 128-step window.

loss = policy_loss − 0.001·entropy + 0.5·value_losses + predictor_loss
  • policy loss — raise probability of positive-advantage actions, lower for negative; PPO's clipping keeps each update small so 16 steps on the same batch stay safe.
  • value losses — both critics are regressed toward what actually happened, improving the next batch's baselines.
  • predictor loss — the RND predictor trains on the same frames (Fig. 2): the same backward pass that improves the policy also consumes the novelty it chased.

4 · The implanted noise patch

A synthetic "noisy TV": a strip of pure pixel noise stamped into the observation after preprocessing — the agent's eyes see it, the game itself is untouched.

processed frame, 84×84
patch stamped over rows 0–11
what RND then sees (whitened): the patch region never stops being surprising
  • Noise type: every pixel drawn independently and uniformly from 0–255 — maximal-entropy static, no structure to learn.
  • Size: 12×84 px = 14.3% of the frame area (chosen hyperparameter; strip position row 0).
  • Side effect: the strip covers the HUD band — score and lives display — so it also removes that information from the RND input.
  • Stays random: each env owns a salted noise RNG that survives episode ends — a fresh patch every episode, the stream never repeats or resets.
